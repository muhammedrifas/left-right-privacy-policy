Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game "Left-Right: Tune Your Brain". The application is designed to completely respect your privacy and operates entirely offline.

Data Collection and Usage

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, user information, or device identifiers. Specifically:

  • Local Storage Only: All game progress, including high scores, unlocked levels, and app preferences (such as sound settings), is stored strictly locally on your device. The developer has absolutely no access to this data.
  • Completely Offline: The application does not require an active internet connection to function and does not communicate with any external servers.
  • No Third-Party Tracking: The application does not contain any advertisements, analytics trackers, or third-party SDKs that monitor your activity or harvest data.

Children's Privacy

Because the app does not collect any personal information whatsoever, it is completely safe for children and fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Changes to This Policy

If we decide to update this application in the future to include online features, we will update this Privacy Policy and request any necessary permissions. However, the current version remains strictly offline and private.

Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the support email address provided on the application's Google Play Store listing.
